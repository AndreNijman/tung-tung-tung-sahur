TUNG TUNG TUNG SAHORROR
the drumming thing walks the streets before dawn
Alone. Gather every offering and carry them back to the surau before the call to Subuh. The thing only sees what it is facing. It always hears panic.

Together. Five to a lobby, one of you is the Tung. The rest carry offerings home one at a time. The Tung cannot see in colour and cannot hear well — it reads the footprints you left a moment ago. Sprinting leaves a brighter, longer-lasting trail. Standing still leaves none.

Alcoves come in pairs. Hide in one and you can slip through to its partner, so camping a door is a waste of the Tung's night.
